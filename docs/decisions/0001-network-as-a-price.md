# Network as a price

One unconstrained root node through milestone 3. Every home sits under it, and it has no flow limit.

Transformers and their limits arrive in milestone 4, inside `Network`. They do not become a second channel into the home.

A home never reads a node. The only signal it receives from the network is the per-slot price adjustment `a_t` in `docs/formulation.md`. The tariff bill stays the Agile price alone.
